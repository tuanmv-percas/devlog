# ant-hustle

```
1. Spawner

AntSpawner.cs — [NEW]
- Added AntSpawner under Road (scales with CameraFit), running the ant colony in play mode only
- Built the ant queue from LevelDetail.Ants (colour, count) groups; a level without groups gets one group per colour sized to that colour's total block capacity, repeated until the road is full
- Laid out the road as fixed columns rowSpacing apart that move along the spline like a sushi belt, with a distance-to-percent table keeping columns evenly spaced; each column holds one row of up to rowWidth ants of one colour
- Added the gate at the road point nearest the dock: ants matching a docked block leave the road, prefer the block with fewer ants still needed (then the left slot), ring it, count the label down, and at capacity pull the block off screen and release the slot
- Added win (no block left on board, dock or being pulled) and lose (dock full, no ant walking to a block, no matching ant on the road, hole cannot release more); both publish to IEventService when present, otherwise log
- Moved the hole-to-road steering into Crawl and reused it for the road-to-block leg: turn rate limited by turnSpeed, turn in place past walkTurnLimit, Perlin wander calming near the goal, path bending around the hole mouth, distance measured on the road plane
- Made ants merge onto the road from its inner edge and step sideways into their lane (mergeBehind, mergeSlope), and step out to the outer edge at the gate the same way
- Replaced heading-bending avoidance with a separation shove within separationRadius, smoothed over time; heading kept in [-pi, pi)
- Moved the edge path to 1.05 from belt center and clamped the inner offset to 85% of curve radius on tight curves so ant feet stay off the rail; edge spacing uses real ground distance
- Replaced per-ant hole steering with a fixed spiral trail built at spawn: ants follow it by distance in single file, one body length apart, and meet the inner road edge tangentially
- Waiting ants form a ring around the rim that slides toward the exit; a row claims its column when its first ant nears the merge point, and the line stops before the merge when no column is free
- Removed the edge passing lane and the scatter/hold logic
- Split the trail into up to maxArms spiral arms, each with its own rim exit, waiting arc and merge point; arm count and length derive from the narrowest hole-to-road gap so arms stay armSpacing apart and turn at least minArmTurns; new rows go to the arm with the fewest waiting ants
- Restricted merge points to edge stretches visible from the hole, running with the spiral and away from sharp corners; each arm is a spiral body plus a lead-in parallel to the edge, relaxed with Taubin smoothing and clamped between the hole rim and the visible edge
- Moved the trail lead-in start to 0.35 inside the edge to join the spiral body (0.3 margin) on the new stadium road
- Limited lane step-in time so heading stays within stepAngle (35 degrees) of the road, with a quintic step-in curve and along-road speed carried over from the edge
- Added a braking curve for chasing the column on the edge so ants arrive at belt speed before stepping in
- Made ants climb out of the hole from rest with exitAcceleration, blending from the waiting pose
- Added speed easing before tight spiral bends to keep turning under trailTurnRate (300 degrees per second)

RoadSpawner.cs — [MOD]
- Exposed HoleWorldPosition and HoleMouthRadius for the ant spawner
- Replaced level-driven road with a fixed closed Bezier stadium (two straights joined by half circles, set in Shape fields), built the same for every level, with the hole placed at its center
- Removed the belt; fit points now come from the outer rail mesh only

- Dropped the default road shape from LevelDetail.Road; the field stays only so saved levels still load

Block.cs — [MOD]

BlockVisual.cs — [MOD]
```
