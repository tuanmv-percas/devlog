# color-sand-block

1. Hidden Block

HiddenFeature.cs — [MOD]
- Replaced the pop-and-shrink reveal with a card flip: the cover tenses for 0.06s (scale 0.96, lid flash 0.1, mark pulse 0.3, mark wobble 18°), turns 90° to edge on, then the block's holder turns in from -90° with an OutBack overshoot over a total of 0.32s
- Added a 0.6-square lift towards the camera at the middle of the turn, and a 0.1s settle hold before the feature leaves
- Added PoseCover() to scale, turn and shift the cover about its footprint centre in its parent's space
- Added TurnOwner() / UnturnOwner() so the turn is applied on top of the holder's current pose and taken back off exactly, including when the reveal is cut short or the block is being dragged
- Added Land() to play the hidden_reveal sound (only when AudioDataConfig has the entry) and the HiddenReveal haptic at the moment the block shows
- Added HideCover() to switch off the showing cover model instead of scaling it to zero
- Added per-renderer _Flash / _MarkPulse / _Wobble pushes through the lid MaterialPropertyBlock
- The counter label now refreshes only while the block is still hidden, and scales out with InBack during the tense beat

HiddenLid.shader — [MOD]
- Added reveal inputs _Flash, _MarkPulse, _Wobble (per renderer) and _FlashColor, _WobbleSpeed (material)
- Marks now rock around their own tilt and swell with the pulse, clamped to the room left in their slot so they are never cut
- Renderers with no _MarkSpace (the cover body) no longer take marks, so the body can share the shader and flash with the lid

hidden.mat — [MOD]
- Switched to the Percas/HiddenLid shader with _MarkChance 0, so the body flashes together with the lid

hidden_body.png.meta — [MOD]
- Set max texture size to 512

hidden.prefab — [MOD]
- Pointed the counter text renderers at the new hidden_count font material

LilitaOne-Regular Atlas Material_hidden_count.mat — [NEW]
- Own font material for the hidden counter: white face with a dark grey outline, leaving the shared LilitaOne material untouched

HiddenVFXElement.cs, HiddenVfxBuilder.cs, HiddenParticle.shader, HiddenVFXElement.prefab, Arts/Vfx/hidden/*.mat — [NEW, then DEL]
- Built a pooled reveal burst (puff, 18 question marks, block-coloured sparks, ring, glow) with an editor builder under Tools > Color Sand Block > Build Hidden Reveal VFX
- Removed the same day when the reveal moved to the card flip; the PrefabManager.hiddenParticle slot added for it was removed with it

2. Sticky Block

StickyFeature.cs — [MOD]
- Staples now break on the board as soon as either block of the pair clears, one after another along the seam (0.035s stagger), each squeezing to 0.82 for 0.06s before it shatters
- Added BreakPins(), which holds the cleared block's launch until the last staple has broken plus a 0.15s beat
- Added HandleCompletedDuringBreak(): if the partner block clears while the staples are still breaking, it holds its launch for the remaining time so both launch together
- Added PlayPop() to spawn the pooled StickyVFXElement at each staple, tinted to the staple colour, and play the StickyBreak haptic on the first break
- Added m_SnapVfx field and the _pins / _pinColour state, with ClearPins() on clean-up
- Removed the old shrink-to-zero connector animation and the animate flag from ClearConnectors()

StickyVFXElement.cs — [NEW]
- Pooled chip burst with Tint() and Play(), used for the staple break

sticky.fbx, sticky.mat — [NEW] / [MOD]
- Added the staple model; the connector pivot sits at the staple's feet at block wall height, with the art as a child

TextureArray.asset — [NEW]
- Per-colour Texture2DArray for the staple, built from textures 1.png to 28.png
- Raised depth from 14 to 28 layers so staples on the colours added since (DarkRed to Charcoal) no longer sample outside the array

1.png ... 28.png — [NEW]
- Staple textures, one per sand colour

sticky.prefab — [MOD]
- Wired the staple model into the connector and seated it on the block rims; assigned the snap VFX

StickyVFXElement.prefab, Arts/Vfx/sticky/chip.mat, chip.png — [NEW]
- Chip burst prefab and its material and texture

BlockRisenSignal.cs — [NEW, then DEL]
- Published from the complete animation once the block had risen, so staples could ride up and break at the top of the launch
- Removed when the staples moved to breaking on the board before launch; the risen callback on BlockCompleteAnimation was removed with it (no net change to that file)
