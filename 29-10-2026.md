4. Conveyor Road

RoadData.cs — [NEW]
- Per-level road shape stored as a Dreamteck SplinePoint[] with SampleRate and spline Type, in board-local coordinates
- ApplyTo(spline) forces every point onto the board plane (z = 0, normal = back) so the belt faces the camera
- Roads are always closed loops; MinPoints = 3, HasShape requires at least 3 points
- CreateDefault() returns a 4-point BSpline circle centered above the dock for new levels

RoadSpawner.cs — [NEW]
- Builds the road from RoadData into a belt (PathGenerator) and two rails (TubeGenerator) on a shared SplineComputer; these are fixed scene objects reused across levels, not pooled
- Scrolls the belt UV by distance travelled (beltSpeed 4, materialMoveScale 0.5) through a MaterialPropertyBlock so the shared material asset is not modified
- Clear() disables the generators and empties the spline without destroying the fixed children
- Added Rebuild(road) and IsBuilt for the Level Editor to preview drags without touching level data

LevelDetail.cs — [MOD]
- Added RoadData Road, defaulting to RoadData.CreateDefault()
- BoardData now defaults to new(); Size defaults to 3x3

GridPlaceholderBuilder.cs — [MOD]
- Added SetupRoad: creates the Road spawner, Spline, Belt and RailLeft / RailRight generators with Bus Traffic Fever MainConveyor parameters scaled by 0.7
- Moved the camera back to (0, -5, -24) looking at (0, 2.4, 0) to frame board, dock and road
