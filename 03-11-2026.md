# ant-hustle

```
1. Spawner

AntSpawner.cs — [MOD]
- Moved the gate from the road point nearest the dock center to the door (GateDistance), GateLead before the door middle; GateLead raised from 0.25 to 0.45 for the slower step-out
- Replaced fixed stepDuration with stepSideSpeed (3.5): step-out sideways speed ramps up over 0.2 s (StepOutRamp) then holds; step-in time derives from stepSideSpeed (StepInTime)
- Added walkAcceleration (8): ants start walking with the heading and speed they left the road with, carry the frame's leftover time into the walk, and slow down to turn instead of turning in place
- Added braking toward the block so ants reach joinRadius at JoinSpeed 1.5, then a Hermite glide onto the attach point from walking velocity to rest, with the turn capped at SettleTurnRate 360 deg/s
- Normalized the spline forward/right used for lane facing (forward was 0.6 long)
- When only part of a row leaves, the ants nearest the rail go first so leaving ants no longer walk through staying ones
- Replaced pullDuration with a carry path (CarryPath): end-slot blocks go straight out to their side; middle-slot blocks always drop into the gap below the dock first (CarryDodge, CarryReach), then go out to the nearer side
- Triggered RoadSpawner.PlayDoor when a row walks out of the door
- Climb out of the hole starts at 35% of crawlSpeed (was 60%) and closes the offset from the peek pose over the first 0.35 of the trail (ClimbBlend) instead of snapping onto it
- Replaced peekTilt with LipPose: waiting ants cling to the inside wall with only the head over the rim, then tip over the lip onto the ground (ClimbOut path segment)
- Added peekGap (0.55): peek slots per arm are capped by how many fit on the mouth ring at the new model's width
- Rising ants keep followGap behind the ant climbing out and hold their slot angle until there is room; followGap raised from 0.7 to 0.9, climbLength from 0.6 to 0.5, PeekBodyLength to 0.74
- Replaced attachRadius / attachDepth with attachEdge (0.42): ants attach on the block's square outline, heads under it, at walking height

RoadSpawner.cs — [MOD]
- Added doorWidth (2) and DoorPercent; OpenDoor clips every rail around the door so the outer rail has an opening at the middle of the bottom straight
- Started the stadium spline at the door point so the clip does not wrap around the spline end
- Added doorPosts: two posts placed on the rail ends at the door, 1.5x the rail size
- Added PlayDoor(delay): LitMotion punch on the post scale (doorPulse 0.35)

----------------------------------------------------------------------

2. Ants

Ant.cs — [MOD]
- Added a tripod gait (three leg bars swinging, body sway) driven by speed measured in LateUpdate, plus PlayLand squash on attach
- Replaced the leg-bar gait with a vertex animation texture: a single body MeshRenderer, skin slice picked by block colour name (skinNames), run/idle frames set through a MaterialPropertyBlock
- Run clip advances with distance actually moved (strideLength 0.9, maxStepRate 5) and blends to idle below idleBelow over blendTime

----------------------------------------------------------------------

3. Dock

DockSlot.cs — [MOD]
- Added PlayLand: slot dips by landDip (0.08) when a block lands
- Added PlayRelease: slot pops by releasePop (0.15) when it is freed

----------------------------------------------------------------------

4. Entities / Data

Block.cs — [MOD]
- Calls slot.PlayLand() when the jump onto the dock slot completes, before raising BlockDocked

BlockVisual.cs — [MOD]
- Added land squash (landSquash), attach pulse on the root (attachPulse) and a count label pop (countPulse) through PlayAttach
- Changed PlayPullAway to take a waypoint list: lift with a shake (liftShake), then constant carrySpeed after carryRamp, with bob (carryBob, carryBobs) and lean toward the travel side (carryTilt)
- Added a slowCorners flag to BuildPath so carry paths keep speed through corners
- Lowered liftHeight from 0.35 to 0.05 and carryBob from 0.06 to 0.04; only the Root lifts, without the OutBack overshoot, and bobs toward the camera

----------------------------------------------------------------------

5. View

CameraFit.cs — [MOD]
- Added dockRaise (0.08): dock sits 8% of the free space above the midpoint between road and board, leaving a gap below it for carried blocks

----------------------------------------------------------------------

6. Managers

- Rebuilds on EventService.OnReloadLevel so replay and Next work without reloading the scene; clears the level on destroy
- Logs an error when the database is empty or has an empty level entry

----------------------------------------------------------------------

7. Art

AntVatBaker.cs — [NEW]

- Imports AntSkins.png as a 4x3 Texture2DArray and writes AntVat.mat with GPU instancing and render queue 2003

GridPlaceholderBuilder.cs — [MOD]
- Builds the wall from the Models/Wall FBX pieces (WallMesh) with WallBaked.mat instead of the green lit Wall material
- Skips Ant.fbx when reconfiguring model importers so its clips stay enabled for the baker

AntVat.shader — [NEW]
- Added Gameplay/Ant VAT (URP, Geometry+3): samples vertex positions from the VAT and the body colour from the _Skins array by an instanced _Skin slice

DepthMask.shader — [MOD]
- Updated the header to describe the mask as a ring around the hole mouth instead of a disc over it

AntVat.mat, AntVatMesh.asset, AntVat.asset, AntSkins.png — [NEW]
- Baked VAT material, mesh, position texture and packed skin atlas

Ant.fbx, Ant_01_Black.png ... Ant_12_Red.png — [NEW]
- Added the rigged ant model with run/idle clips and its 12 body colour textures

WallBaked.mat — [NEW]
- URP Unlit material on Wall_BaseColor.png, since the texture has shading baked in

Wall.mat, Wall.fbx — [DEL]
- Removed the old green wall material and single-file wall model, replaced by WallBaked.mat and the Models/Wall pieces

WallBlock / WallCorner / WallFillet / WallMiddle / WallPlane / WallRight / WallRim.fbx, Wall_BaseColor.png — [NEW]

hole_mouth_ring.asset — [NEW]
```
