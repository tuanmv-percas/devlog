# sand-trip

```
1. Track

Scripts/GamePlay/Track/ITrackPath.cs — [NEW]
- Declared a closed rail loop measured by arc length, with Evaluate, Project and TryRaycast on the XY plane; distances outside [0, Length) wrap around the loop

Scripts/GamePlay/Track/PolylineTrackPath.cs — [NEW]
- Implemented ITrackPath over a list of straight segments; the last point joins back to the first and consecutive duplicate points are dropped

Scripts/GamePlay/Track/ITrackPointSource.cs — [NEW]
- Declared the source of rail points, in travel order and world coordinates, so the rail shape is swapped by swapping a component

Scripts/GamePlay/Track/DreamteckSplineTrackSource.cs — [NEW]
- Sampled the rail loop from a Dreamteck SplineComputer; compiled only under the DREAMTECK_SPLINES define

Scripts/GamePlay/Track/RectTrackSource.cs — [NEW]
- Generated a rounded-rectangle rail loop on the transform's XY plane, centred on its origin

Scripts/GamePlay/Track/WaypointTrackSource.cs — [NEW]
- Built the rail loop through the object's child transforms in hierarchy order, closing the last child back to the first

Scripts/GamePlay/Track/TrackPose.cs — [NEW]
- Added a struct holding a position on the rail and the travel direction there

Scripts/GamePlay/Track/TrackView.cs — [NEW]
- Built the ITrackPath from the point source on the same object in Awake, registered it in ServiceLocator for the scene's lifetime and drew the rail gizmo

2. Station

Scripts/GamePlay/Station/StationService.cs — [NEW]
- Reserved a station seat when a box is tapped, counting boxes still on the way toward the capacity of 5
- Queued arrived boxes FIFO by arrival order, with queue [0] as the bottom box
- Added close/open and cleanup on OnReloadLevel and on Gameplay scene unload

Scripts/GamePlay/Station/StationView.cs — [NEW]
- Stacked boxes from StackAnchor by StackStep, hopped each arriving box into its place and placed off-screen boxes at EntryPoint first
- Showed the "count/capacity" text on the tunnel front and punched it once on StationRefusedSignal

Scripts/GamePlay/Station/StationConfigSO.cs — [NEW]
- Added capacity and hop timings of the station as placeholder values to tune (GDD Q-13)

----------------------------------------------------------------------

3. Train

Scripts/GamePlay/Train/TrainService.cs — [NEW]
- Moved the train head around the rail with each slot at a fixed distance behind it; Tick is driven by TrainView
- Picked the bottom station box into an empty slot early, when the slot is exactly BoardHopDuration of travel away from the pickup point, so the box lands as the car arrives
- Raised the lose condition: all 5 slots full and one full lap with no NotifySandCollected slows the train to a stop, closes the station and calls IEventService.PublishLevelLost()
- Exposed PickupDistance, the rail distance of the pickup point

Scripts/GamePlay/Train/TrainSpeedController.cs — [NEW]
- Applied the GDD speed table in order: slow to a stop in 1 s on lose, ramp to 200% in 0.5 s on hold or cleared board, return to 100% in 0.5 s otherwise; every change is linear

Scripts/GamePlay/Train/IdleLapTracker.cs — [NEW]
- Counted rail distance travelled while every slot holds a box and no sand was collected, reporting the lose exactly once after one lap

Scripts/GamePlay/Train/HoldGesture.cs — [NEW]
- Detected a continuous hold anywhere outside UI longer than 0.5 s, released immediately on lift

Scripts/GamePlay/Train/TrainSlot.cs — [NEW]
- Added a car slot with its fixed offset behind the head and the box it carries

Scripts/GamePlay/Train/WhistleTimer.cs — [NEW]
- Timed the whistle at random intervals around a mean, counting only while the train runs

Scripts/GamePlay/Train/TrainView.cs — [NEW]
- Spawned the cars, bound TrainService to TrackView, placed head and cars along the rail each frame, hopped boxes from the station onto cars, fired the whistle and enabled hold-to-speed-up

Scripts/GamePlay/Train/TrainCarView.cs — [NEW]
- Added BoxHolder, the transform a boarded box is parented to with zero local position/rotation and scale 1

Scripts/GamePlay/Train/TrainConfigSO.cs — [NEW]
- Added slot count, FirstSlotGap 1.51, SlotSpacing 1.21, DefaultSpeed 3.27 (Marble Hole values x 0.327), speed multipliers and boarding timings BoardHopDuration, BoardSettleDuration 0.15, RestackDelay 0.25

4. Sand Suction

Scripts/GamePlay/Sand/SandSuctionService.cs — [NEW]
- Sucked grains for each box on the train: when the search zone holds sand of the box colour, pulled grains from the larger suck zone until the box reaches its capacity
- Skipped sucking and clearing until the box has landed on its car and while it is inside the red zone (a stretch of rail around the pickup)
- Cleared a full box outside the red zone after the clear timer, freed its slot and despawned it
- Called TrainService.NotifySandCollected on every suck and published the win once the picture is empty and the train carries no box

Scripts/GamePlay/Sand/OrientedRectSuction.cs — [NEW]
- Added a suction rectangle that turns with the box, near edge parallel to the rail at Gap from Origin, scanned line by line from the rail inward and limited to one colour
- Added HasAny to test the zone for a suckable grain without touching the grid

Scripts/GamePlay/Sand/SandSuctionView.cs — [NEW]
- Bound the service to the SandPictureView board, ticked it each frame and flew sucked grains to their box through GrainFlight particles
- Played the shrink animation on BoxClearingSignal and drew gizmos for the search zone, suck zone and red zone

Scripts/GamePlay/Sand/BoxSandProgress.cs — [NEW]
- Tracked per-box Need, Sucked, fractional Pending grains, HopTimeLeft before landing and the clear state/timer

Scripts/GamePlay/Sand/BoxCapacityTable.cs — [NEW]
- Moved the capacity split to runtime: cells of a colour on the picture divided evenly among boxes of that colour, remainder to the first box in BoardData.Occupied order

Scripts/GamePlay/Sand/SandSuctionConfigSO.cs — [NEW]
- Added zone sizes, suck rate, red zone length and clear timing as placeholder values to tune (GDD Q-13)
```
