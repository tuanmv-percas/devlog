1. Shadows

Scripts/SnakeFlow/Shadows/IElementShadow.cs — [NEW]
- New interface for any fake shadow gameplay can drive: SetAlpha(float), SetLift(Vector3) and SetScale(float)
- SetLift takes the world offset the shadow does not follow, so a lifted object leaves its shadow at the resting spot

Scripts/SnakeFlow/Shadows/FakeShadow.cs — [MOD]
- Implements IElementShadow; SetAlpha now uses inheritdoc with the renderer-off note moved to remarks
- Added _lift and SetLift: SyncShadow subtracts _lift from the pinned position, and SetLift re-syncs by hand because m_Static disables LateUpdate
- Added _scale and SetScale: the shadow localScale becomes m_ScaleMultiplier * _scale, applied both at creation and on change

Scripts/SnakeFlow/Shadows/SpriteShadow.cs — [MOD]
- Implements IElementShadow so the SpriteShadow under the Block's Box node is now driven alongside FakeShadow
- Added SetAlpha through a MaterialPropertyBlock on _Color: alpha 0 disables the renderer, alpha 1 clears the block to keep GPU instancing
- Added SetLift/ApplyLift: quad localPosition = m_LocalOffset minus the lift converted into the source's local space
- Added SetScale against _baseScale captured in CreateShadow, and _baseColor read from m_ShadowMaterial
- Awake applies lift, scale and alpha so values set before Awake are kept

Scripts/SnakeFlow/Columns/Elements/BlockElement.cs — [MOD]

- Hover rise and bob switched from Tween.LocalPosition to Tween.Custom through ApplyHoverPosition, which moves the model then re-pins the shadow

- Added ApplyShadowLift / ClearShadowLift / SetShadowLift: shadows stay anchored at the parked spot by HoverShadowAnchor of the model's lift; cleared on fly-off and on pose reset
- Added shadow scale tweening (_shadowScale, _shadowScaleFrom/_shadowScaleTo, ApplyShadowScale) alongside the existing alpha tween in the collect pose and the glide into the slot

Scripts/GamePlay/ArrowPicture/Arrow.Visual.cs — [MOD]
- Added RoundTightCorners: any corner whose adjacent segment is no longer than bodyWidth is replaced with a pre-sampled arc (TightCornerStepsPer90 = 8), so LineRenderer only sees joints of a few degrees
- Arc radius is half the body width, shrunk to the free part of each segment; a segment shared by two corners is split between them, turning the bottom of the U into the hole into a clean semicircle
- Fixes the pointed wedge at the U-turn into the hole and at head/tail just past a corner; normal corners and head/tail/limb/motif positions are unchanged
- Added IsCorner and EnsureRoundedCapacity helpers
