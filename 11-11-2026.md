# color-sand-block

```
1. Boosters — Commands & Selection

MagnetBoosterCommand.cs — [NEW]
- Added Magnet booster: player picks a block still owing sand, and its targetFill is pulled from the pictures (nearest grains first, then their pipes, then covered pictures) and flies in through BlockElement.FillSand
- Valid targets exclude ice, lock, hidden and double-colour blocks for now
- Effect waits for the block to close; tutorial accepts only the block the hand points at
- Board is measured on its visible renderers (no shadow, no sand pipes); taps are taken while the camera moves, only the tutorial waits for the camera before placing the hand
- Moved targets, spotlight selection and sand pull into BlockFillBoosterCommand; Magnet now only picks the single block

BlockFillBoosterCommand.cs — [NEW]
- New abstract base for boosters that fill blocks from the pictures (shared by Magnet and Vacuum)
- FillAsync pulls grains for a list of blocks through one buffer and waits for all of them to land
- Landing wait times out only after 6 s with no grain landing (was a fixed 6 s total) and returns false when Clear cuts it, so a board torn down mid-effect refunds the booster

VacuumBoosterCommand.cs — [NEW]
- Added Vacuum booster: the picked block, then every other valid block of its colour in board order, takes its targetFill from the pictures and closes as usual
- Same-colour blocks that are not valid targets (door, lock, hidden, ice, double-colour) are skipped and their share stays in the picture, pending design

BoosterSelection.cs — [MOD]
- Added Spotlight(lit) to light the targets with a ring in place of the dark sheet, so a tap anywhere is reported (miss = cancel without charge)
- Added FocusBoardAsync: banner sits on top of the board and the camera moves so banner + board are centred in the safe area; when they do not fit, the board goes to the bottom and the banner overlaps its top
- Camera returns on Dispose only if nothing else moved it since
```
