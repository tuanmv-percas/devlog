1. Ice Machine — Gameplay

IceMachineElement.cs — [MOD]
- Ice laid out one piece per cell from three hidden templates (m_IceHead, m_IceMiddle, m_IceTail) instead of one stretched mesh; a pull slides the bar into the mouth while the head piece squashes in
- m_PullDuration raised from 0.28 s to 0.55 s
- Added Grinder: the roller turns m_GrinderTurns whole turns per cell pulled and dips onto the bar (m_GrinderDip 0.04/0/0.12 cells, m_GrinderDipTime 0.2 of the pull)
- Added effect slots m_BiteEffect (at the roller), m_SprayEffect (hole on top), m_IceIdle (loop over the bar still out, stretched to its length); without m_BiteEffect the shared ice burst plays at the mouth
- Vanish rebuilt: wind-up m_VanishWindUp 0.3 s where the machine swells (m_VanishSwell 0.1), shakes (m_VanishShake 0.025 cells), the roller spins 720 degrees and the top sprays once; then m_VanishEffect burst, a 0.07 s squash and a shrink with a 200 degree twist (m_VanishTwist)
- Added intro: listens for BoardIntroSignal, hides on Hide, and on Play comes up after m_IntroDelay 0.2 s with a squash-and-stretch pop (m_IntroPop 0.45 s) and a landing puff, then runs its bar out of the mouth at m_IntroCellTime 0.16 s per cell while the roller turns backwards

IceMachineSetup.cs — [MOD]

- Every colour derived from one wood hue (FreshWood, WoodTop, Sawdust, SawdustDeep, DustFresh, DustSettled)
- Particle materials on Legacy Shaders/Particles Alpha Blended and Additive, the same shaders the board's other ice bursts use
