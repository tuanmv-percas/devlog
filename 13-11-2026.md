1. Boosters / Magnet Fx

MagnetView.cs — [MOD]
- Screen dims behind the magnet during the fly-in (0.2 s fade in to DimAlpha 0.72), holds through the spin, fades out over 0.25 s as the magnet starts down onto the block
- Dim is an Image on an overlay canvas sorted above the HUD; the magnet renders through its own camera into a RenderTexture shown on that canvas, then hands back to the main camera on landing for the field's GrabPass
- Scale follows the same eased clock as position: button size at launch, grows to SpinScale on the rise, holds through the spin, shrinks back to 1x on the drop
- Fly-in starts at the Magnet button's size (ButtonFit, measured from the art's mesh bounds) and centred on the button
- Button point and mid-screen turn point are recomputed every frame while the camera eases back from the target focus
- Magnet waits hidden for LaunchDelaySeconds (0.3 s) before leaving the button so it does not come out of a sliding board; a cut during the wait leaves it hidden
- Spawns MagnetLightning that sparks off the magnet's mesh, runs unbroken from the button to the vanish, drops to PullLightningRate (0.5) while the sand streams
- Vanish now swells to VanishPunch while every bolt and arc fires, spawns MagnetBurst in world space, then shrinks away over 0.12 s
- Plays booster_magnet on its own AudioSource at the scene root when the magnet appears, fades it over SoundFadeOutSeconds (0.4 s) on vanish or cut; plays booster_magnet_burst on the burst
- Overlay RawImage uses Percas/OverlayPremultiplied so additive particles show over the dim
- SparkOff uses a copy of the mesh with recalculated normals when a build has stripped them (Optimize Mesh Data), so bolts no longer all fly to one side

MagnetArcs.cs — [NEW]
- Draws ArcCount jagged LineRenderers from one magnet leg to the other, redrawn every ArcSeconds
- Leg tips found from the art's mesh; lines parented under the model so they turn and rock with it
- Runs at full count with the lightning and at a share of it while pulling (at least one)

MagnetAnimSettings.cs — [MOD]
- Added LaunchDelaySeconds, ButtonFit, SpinScale (4x)
- Added Dim overlay group: DimAlpha, DimFadeInSeconds, DimFadeOutSeconds, OverlaySortingOrder, OverlayShader
- Added Lightning group (LightningPrefab, LightningScale, PullLightningRate) and Arcs group (ArcMaterial, ArcCount, ArcWidth, ArcJitter, ArcSeconds)
- Added VanishPunch, VanishPunchSeconds, VanishPrefab; VanishSeconds 0.3 -> 0.12
- Added sound fade-out duration for the magnet crackle
- Removed ArriveNearScale and ArriveStartScale

OverlayPremultiplied.shader — [NEW]
- UI overlay shader with Blend One OneMinusSrcAlpha, so a RenderTexture cleared to transparent keeps additive particle colour
