# bus-down

```
1. Piece Movement

PieceGrid.cs — [MOD]
- Added frame interior state (_frameGapRows, _frameColStart, _frameColEnd) and SetFrame(gapRows, firstColumn, lastColumn) so the walkable area above the wall band follows the frame instead of picture + RingPadding
- Added FrameRowStart / FrameRowEnd; CorridorRowStart now starts right below FrameRowEnd
- Frame keeps at least 1 column (MinFrameSideColumns) on each side of the picture when the screen is too narrow
- Corridor through the wall band is centered on local x = 0 with a fractional half-width (_corridorHalfWidth); when its parity differs from the picture width one extra column opens and the two edge columns are walkable only on their inner half
- Replaced TryGetCorridorColumns with TryGetCorridorEdges (float left/right edges in picture cells)
- Added CellToPathPoint(cellIndex, clearance) and ClampToCorridor() to keep the Piece body inside the corridor edges
- Replaced GetGapFirstColumn with IsCorridorColumn(col, gap); IsInsideRing no longer takes firstOpen

PieceMovementService.cs — [MOD]
- Added TryMeasureFrameColumns() to compute frame side columns from the camera viewport, inset by frameGap cells and rounded inward (FrameEdgeEpsilon)
- Grid rebuild takes the camera and calls PieceGrid.SetFrame; falls back to picture + RingPadding when no camera is available
- Cell-center path points pulled inside the corridor so Pieces do not clip the visible wall

PieceMovementConfig.cs — [MOD]
- Added frameGap = 3 (cells kept between frame and grid top / wall band / screen edges)
- Rewrote bottomWallGapWidth doc for the centered, parity-aware corridor
```
