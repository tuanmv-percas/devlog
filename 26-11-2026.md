# sand-trip

```
1. Sand Simulation

SandStepJob.cs — [MOD]
- Ported Sand Block's sand steer: a hole under the sand is filled from the side whose surface is higher (averaged over SteerRadius columns, past SteerTolerance) instead of always from the nearest wall
- Added Surface struct that measures every column's sand height once per step as prefix sums, so each side average is constant time
- Randomized the split column where the left and right sweeps meet within SteerRadius so no sand pillar stays at the middle
- Stepped only the columns inside each row's activeSpans and cleared the span before stepping the row
- Marked both cells of every slide, mix and steer move active for the next step (Moved helper)
- Computed the highest occupied row with a top-down scan (TopRow) instead of tracking it per grain

GridPicture.cs — [MOD]
- Added activeSpans (one column span per row) and NoSpan constant
- SetGrain and ClearGrain now mark the cell and its neighbours active through MarkActive
- Added MarkAllActive, called after the picture is filled; disposed activeSpans with the other arrays

SandPictureSimulation.cs — [MOD]
- Added sleep at rest: after a step pass where no grain moved, steps stop being scheduled until suction takes grains (WakeUp)
- Passed SteerRadius and SteerTolerance into SandStepJob
- Widened the step seed range from 1–255 to 1–int.MaxValue
- Called JobHandle.ScheduleBatchedJobs right after scheduling so the step chain starts on workers immediately

SandConfigSO.cs — [MOD]
- Added SteerRadius (default 8) and SteerTolerance (default 2) under a Steer header

CollectColorJob.cs — [MOD]
- Collect now repeats passes over the mouth lines until capacity is reached or a pass takes nothing, so suction reaches the configured rate
- Lowered the column height after every taken grain on all mouth edges, not only the side edges

----------------------------------------------------------------------

2. Grain Flight

GrainFlight.cs — [MOD]
- Each emitted particle waits a random part of the frame before flying, so a suction batch no longer leaves as one row
- Applied per-side LifetimeJitter to each particle's flight time and stored flight times in _lifetimes instead of reading startLifetime

GrainFlightProfile.cs — [MOD]
- Added LifetimeJitter (0–0.9, default 0.35)

StationView.cs — [MOD]

4. Picture Frame & Rail

PictureFrameView.cs — [NEW]
- Builds a rounded, raised frame mesh around the sand picture: front face submesh and inner/outer side submesh
- Inner size follows the picture's Frame; inner corner radius follows SandPictureView.CornerRadius

FrameTrackShape.cs — [NEW]
- Editor-only: bends the rail SplineComputer into a rounded rectangle around the frame with an even gap (more on top/bottom for the tilted camera)
- Splits the rail mesh into one channel per spline segment with a whole number of tiles, so all four corners match and sleepers stay evenly spaced
- Compiled only with DREAMTECK_SPLINES; records undo and prefab modifications

RoundedRect.cs — [NEW]
```
