# sand-trip

```
1. Gameplay Elements

Scripts/GamePlay/Elements/Features/BoxCoverFeature.cs — [MOD]
- Added the covered-tap reject shake: m_RejectAngle, m_RejectDuration, m_RejectVibrato drive a rotation shake of the obstacle model when BoxTapRejectedSignal hits a box that is still covered
- Added Uncover(bool instant), SwapToBox() and the virtual PlayUncover() / PlayTapRejected(Sequence) hooks so each cover only authors its own motion
- Moved the shared sign pop (m_Sign, m_SignPunch, m_SignPunchDuration, PunchSign) and reveal burst (m_RevealVfx, m_SparkleVfx tinted by m_SparkleWhiten, box grow-in with m_GrowDuration / m_GrowOvershoot, InsertBurst) into the base class
- Added m_ReactDelay, m_PopScale, m_PopDuration exposed as protected properties for subclasses
- Raises BoxUncoveredSignal from AnnounceUncovered() at the burst point

Scripts/GamePlay/Elements/Features/Hidden/HiddenFeature.cs — [MOD]
- Covered tap now punches the ? sign on top of the base shake
- Uncover plays pop, spin (m_VanishSpin), lift (m_VanishLift) and collapse of the cover, then fires the burst at m_BurstPoint
- Removed own sign/vfx/grow fields after they moved into BoxCoverFeature; m_UncoverDelay became the base m_ReactDelay

Scripts/GamePlay/Elements/Features/Ice/IceFeature.cs — [MOD]
- An Ice box with zero ice now opens instantly at spawn without feedback
- Each crack lands one beat after the collect: punches the ice block (m_CrackPunch, m_CrackDuration, m_CrackVibrato), pops the count (m_CountPunch) and plays m_CrackVfx; raises IceCrackedSignal
- At zero ice the block swells and trembles (m_TrembleDuration, m_TrembleStrength, m_TrembleVibrato) before the shared shatter burst
- Covered tap now pops the ice count like the Hidden ? sign
- Added Clean() override to kill the crack tween

Scripts/GamePlay/Elements/Stack/StackElement.cs — [MOD]
- Added m_Root and public Root so a bumping box can knock the stack
- Push squashes the stack along its arrow (m_PushAnticipation, m_PushSquash) then springs back with overshoot (m_PushRecoilDuration, m_PushRecoilOvershoot) in PlayPushRecoil()
- PlayBoxOut() spawns the box at the stack mouth at m_PushStartScale, grows it along its travel and squashes it on landing (m_LandSquash, m_LandDuration)
- Count pops on each push and m_PushVfx puffs dust sideways from the mouth; raises StackPushedSignal
- Retire() waits for the last push, pops, spins and shrinks, frees the cell as it vanishes, plays m_RetireVfx and despawns after m_RetireVfxLifetime; raises StackClearedSignal
- Added ResetVisual() for pooled reuse

Scripts/GamePlay/Elements/Lock/LockElement.cs — [MOD]
- Added m_Root (public Root) for bump knocks and a rattle on bump (m_RattleAngle, m_RattleDuration, m_RattleVibrato)
- Added m_Keyhole and KeyholePosition at the depth of the lock body for the key insert
- Unlock click: squash (m_ClickSquash, m_ClickDuration), keyhole flash m_UnlockVfx, shackle clip played at m_AnimSpeed and held on the fully open frame; raises LockUnlockedSignal
- Retire pops, spins and shrinks, frees the cell, plays m_RetireVfx and despawns after m_RetireVfxLifetime; raises LockClearedSignal
- m_TintedVfx are tinted with the pair colour, lifted by m_TintWhiten

Scripts/GamePlay/Elements/Features/Key/KeyFeature.cs — [MOD]
- Idle key sways (m_SwayAngle, m_SwayDuration) with pair-coloured twinkles from m_IdleVfx
- Launch pops the key off the box (m_LaunchPop, m_LaunchDuration, m_LaunchLift), spins it along the jump (m_FlySpin) with m_TrailVfx and brightens it toward white (m_ArriveWhiten, m_ArriveScale)
- ApplyFlightPose() tips the teeth into the lock face over the last m_AimShare of the flight
- AppendUnlock() drives the key straight in along the lock z axis (m_InsertOffset, m_InsertDepth, m_InsertDuration), turns it m_TurnAngle and shrinks it away; raises KeyLaunchedSignal

Scripts/GamePlay/Elements/Box/BoxElement.cs — [MOD]
- Added public Color property resolved from the palette by ColorId
- Sets the _Index shader property from ColorId to pick the slice in BoxTextureArray
```
