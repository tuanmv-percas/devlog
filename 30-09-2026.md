2. Elements

Scripts/SnakeFlow/Elements/BlockBeamVisual.cs — [NEW]
- Abduction beam component: builds a slanted truncated cone mesh whose apex sits at the block emitter and whose base disc lies on the slot floor, so the disc stays put while the block bobs
- BuildCone writes vertices, normals, uv, uv2 and vertex colour in one pass and rebuilds only when segments, base centre or either radius changed; buffers are reallocated only on a segment-count change
- Vertex colour tags the shaft (r = 1) apart from the pool disc (r = 0), and the triangle list is split into _poolTriangles and _shaftTriangles
- Show(config, baseCenter, baseRadius, color) rebuilds the cone, applies motes and colour, then fades _Alpha in and scales the cone up from BeamGrowFrom
- Hide(config) stops mote emission, fades _Alpha out, shrinks the cone back toward its apex and disables the renderer on complete
- Pulse(peak, up, down) runs a Sequence flash, skipped while the fade tween is alive so two tweens never write _Alpha at once
- LateUpdate repositions every live mote by hand along the cone surface from its remaining lifetime: angle from the particle random seed plus BeamMoteSpinTurns, radius shrinking by 1 - t^radialPower and height rising by t^axialPower
- ApplyColor lerps the block colour toward white by BeamColorLift, writes it to the particle system startColor and to _Color through the MaterialPropertyBlock, forcing alpha back to 1
- Destroys its generated mesh in OnDestroy and stops every tween before building a new one
